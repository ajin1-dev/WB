Governance:

* Worldwide Governance Indicators (1996-2024)

  * https://data360.worldbank.org/en/int/dataset/WB\_WGI
  * Includes 6 indicators: Control of Corruption, Gov. Effectiveness, Pol. Stability, Rule of Law, Voice and Accountability



* Rule of Law Index (2013-2024) (World Justice Project)

  * https://data360.worldbank.org/en/int/dataset/WJP\_ROL
  * 5 Indicators: Constraints on Gov., Absence of Corruption, Open Gov., Fundamental rights, Order and Security



* Bertelsmann Transformation Index: Governance Index (2006-2026) (BTI)

  * https://data360.worldbank.org/en/int/indicator/BS\_BTI\_G



Debt:

* General Govt Gross Debt, %GDP (1980-2029) (WEO)

  * https://data360.worldbank.org/en/int/indicator/IMF\_WEO\_GGXWDG\_NGDP



* General Govt Net Debt, %GDP (1980-2029) (WEO)

  * https://data360.worldbank.org/en/int/indicator/IMF\_WEO\_GGXWDN\_NGDP



Conflict and Fragility:

* Classification of Fragile and Conflict-Affected Situations (FCS) (list) (2006-2025) (World Bank)

  * https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/b7176d1485821af6f7638e63e266c717-0090082025/original/FCSList-FY06toFY25.pdf
  * Note: Not a dataset; I don't know if there is a dataset using this specific list



* Bertelsmann Transformation Index: Conflict Intensity (2006-2026) (BTI)

  * https://data360.worldbank.org/en/int/indicator/BS\_BTI\_Q13\_3
  * Note: This is on a discrete 0-10 scale, and does not include a number of countries, but these are affluent countries, ie, US, Australia, Sweden



* Forcibly displaced people (2010-2024) (WDI)

  * https://data360.worldbank.org/en/int/indicator/WB\_WDI\_SM\_POP\_FDIP
  * Note: This is a proxy for Conflict and Fragility, and it includes some populations, like asylum-seekers, that cloud it's closeness to Conflict and Fragility



* Index for Risk Management: Vulnerability index (2016-2025) (INFORM)

  * https://data360.worldbank.org/en/int/indicator/INFORM\_VU
  * Note: This is a discrete scale from 0-10, by tenths

Social Assistance:

* Government expenditure on social protection (1972-2023) (IMF)

  * https://data360.worldbank.org/en/int/indicator/IMF\_COFOG\_GESPTO\_GF10
  * Note: This is an official category in the UN's Classification of the Functions of Govt (COFOG)
* Social protection spending, % of GDP (2019-2024) (WEF)

  * https://data360.worldbank.org/en/int/indicator/WEF\_TTDI\_SOCPROTECTOGDP
  * Note: Limited in both time span (2019-2024) and scope (119 countries)
